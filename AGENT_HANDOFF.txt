PRODUCT BOUNDARY
Corroborate is a standalone reusable Intelligent Contract primitive, not a full application.
There must be no frontend, backend, database, dashboard, or off-chain adjudication service.

Corroborate solves source-count inflation in evidence systems. A case freezes a proposition/context and an independent-cluster threshold. Evidence URLs are sealed into an immutable evidence set. GenLayer consensus performs only two substantive semantic jobs:

1. classify each independently fetched source as SUPPORTS, CONTRADICTS, IRRELEVANT, UNAVAILABLE, or AMBIGUOUS relative to the frozen proposition/context;
2. classify every unordered pair of countable evidence as INDEPENDENT, LEFT_DERIVED_FROM_RIGHT, RIGHT_DERIVED_FROM_LEFT, SHARED_ORIGIN, or DEPENDENCE_AMBIGUOUS.

The final corroboration result must remain deterministic. All non-INDEPENDENT pair relations, including DEPENDENCE_AMBIGUOUS, create lineage edges. The contract computes connected components over those edges, aggregates each component to SUPPORT, CONTRADICTION, or MIXED, and applies the frozen threshold. Raw evidence count must never become quorum.

Do not collapse the product into “AI decides whether the proposition is true.” Corroborate measures independent corroboration; it does not claim philosophical truth.
